# Corporate Commercial Performance & Supply Chain Inventory Analytics

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables, Charts, Arithmetic Formulas)

---

## 💡 1. Executive Summary & Core Objectives

This analytical framework audits two primary business ecosystems to identify structural constraints, eliminate margin leakages, and optimize resource deployment across the company's regional and supply pipelines:
1. **Geographic Sales Channels:** Analyzing regional consumer preferences, transactional patterns, and product-specific revenue contribution tiers.
2. **Supply Chain & Inventory Profitability:** Auditing commercial vendor partnerships, stock availability, and individual product markup efficiency.

---

## 🔍 2. Deep-Dive Analytical Modules

### 🌐 Module A: Regional Sales Channels & Demand Vectors
* **The Baseline:** Evaluates **274 physical units sold** across **4 geographic territories** (East, North, South, West), generating **$306,321** in aggregate revenue.
* **Product Insights:**
  * 📱 **Phones (The Core Driver):** Represents the highest-grossing category at **$97,588** (32% of entire revenue pool), with dominant traction across the West ($39,418) and South ($24,228).
  * 💻⌨️ **Laptops & Keyboards:** Delivered steady baseline returns, pulling in **$48,174** and **$60,027** respectively.
  * 🖥️📁 **Monitors & Tablets (The Demand Bottleneck):** Lagged heavily behind other segments, combining for a restricted 16% share of total corporate sales. Additionally, an explicit information gap was flagged for Monitor sales in the North region.

### 📦 Module B: Supply Chain Logistics & Margin Analysis
* **The Baseline:** Details inventory metrics across **592 pieces of stock** distributed among **4 core vendors**, capturing a total projected profit pool of **$244,684**.
* **Vendor Disparities:**
  * **TechSource (The Volume Leader):** Controls 51.6% of warehouse capacity (306 stocks) and accounts for the highest net profit segment (**$94,333**). However, they face heavy profit leakage due to negative markups on Product_2 (-$4,959) and Product_4 (-$29,346).
  * **GigaMall (The Margin Leader):** Displays excellent performance efficiency, bringing in a significant **$75,678** in projected profits with just 2 SKUs by focusing on high-markup lines (Product_9 at an 84.42% profit margin).
  * **DataHub (The Underperforming Tier):** Manages a large footprint of 154 stock pieces but produces a low profit yield ($16,401) due to narrow markups (8.56% on Product_1 and 18.75% on Product_10).

## 🚀 3. Strategic Action Plan

1. **TechSource**: Review product pricing and procurement costs to address losses. Consider removing underperforming items or negotiating better purchase terms to improve margins.
2. **DataHub**: Reassess product pricing strategies — current margins suggest strong potential for scaling. Expanding their presence could increase total profit without significant risk.
3. **ElectroPro**: Conduct a detailed review of marketing and sales performance to identify why their products underperform. Exploring other product lines from this supplier could yield better returns.
4. **GigaMall**: Maintain current strategies — performance is efficient. Future monitoring should focus on sustaining margins as sales volume grows.


**Overall Strategy**: Incorporate regular profit-margin analysis by supplier to identify early warning signs of loss and ensure optimized inventory investment.
